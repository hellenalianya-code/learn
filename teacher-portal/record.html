Tips · Vidokezo

  • Sit in a quiet room: close the door and windows, switch off fans and the radio. Soft things (curtains, a bed, cushions) stop echo.
  • Hold the phone or headset about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth, a little to the side.
  • Read the way you would speak to a class of girls aged 8–14: warm, clear and not too fast.
  • Press Record, wait a moment, read the line, wait a moment, then Stop. Listen back; if it is not right, just record again.
  • Take a break every 20–30 minutes and drink water.
